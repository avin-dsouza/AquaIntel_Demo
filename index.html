Who We Are

Democratizing Water Quality Intelligence

AquaIntel empowers communities to test, understand, and share local water quality data — because clean water is a right, not a privilege.

Contaminated water affects millions of people who don't know the risk. AquaIntel provides affordable test kits and an AI-powered platform that turns your results into actionable insights — telling you if your water is safe to drink, cook with, or use for agriculture.

Every test you submit becomes part of a growing community database. Over time, AquaIntel builds a hyper-local picture of water quality that you can't find anywhere else — revealing patterns, tracking changes, and alerting communities to emerging risks.
